There is a new draft from China. It is a proposal. It concerns export controls. And for the first time... it reaches the weights of AI models. The commerce and science ministries have published this. They are adding twenty-three AI technologies to the restricted-export catalog. This includes large-model weights. It includes checkpoints. It includes source code. And it includes training methods. The controls would trigger under specific conditions. If a model is trained with ten to the power of twenty-six floating-point operations. Or if it has more than two trillion parameters. Or if it holds a top-ten global ranking on major benchmarks. However... there are exemptions. Fully open-source releases are exempt. Content that is already public is exempt. The draft also makes a distinction. It separates access to a model as a product. This means A-P-I calls. Apps. SaaS. From transferring the underlying technology. So, hosted A-P-I use stays uncontrolled. But local overseas deployment of restricted weights or training code... could require a license. Public comment closes on November fifteenth.
