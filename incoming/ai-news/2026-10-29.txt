... So. There is a shift happening. Or rather, a shuffling. Chinese AI labs have stopped trying to siphon data from Anthropic’s Claude. They are now targeting open A-I’s flagship model, GPT-6 Astra. According to The Decoder, DeepSeek and Moonshot AI are leaning heavily on Astra’s outputs. They are using them to train recent models, such as Kimi K3.1 Pro and V-four-point-one Flash, ten-seventeen. Distillation at this depth is a bit... invasive. It requires exposing a model’s hidden reasoning steps. Both open A-I and Anthropic prohibit this. Naturally. Anthropic claims their new defenses have cut the data such attacks can extract by ninety to ninety-nine percent. That is a significant reduction. open A-I, however, reported a coordinated distillation campaign in late September. It was tied to people linked to Moonshot. open A-I says the attackers keep adjusting their tactics. Every time a defense ships, they change their approach. It is a cat and mouse game. And the mouse seems to be winning. This suggests that distillation defense is a moving target. The attacks shift toward whichever lab’s guard is currently weaker. It is not a problem any one company can solve on its own. It is a shared vulnerability. And shared vulnerabilities are rarely handled with much grace. ...
