Attributed to Anthropic and TLDR IT. ... Microsoft confirms a self-propagating AI worm is spreading through Copilot. Security researchers demonstrated a document-borne attack. It hides malicious instructions inside files. These files are processed by Microsoft Copilot. The AI alters generated content. It copies the hidden instructions into new documents. The exploit spreads on its own. It moves across an organization's files. Microsoft has shipped mitigations. Researchers say the root cause remains unfixed. Current models cannot reliably tell untrusted content apart from instructions. They confuse data with commands. ... A worm that propagates by getting an AI to copy its own attack payload is a preview. It is a preview of a new malware category.
