 Business
 
Nvidia strikes a $6B non-acquisition deal for Poolside's AI model-building tech
Nvidia is paying AI startup Poolside $6 billion to non-exclusively license its Model Factory software — the system behind Poolside's Laguna family of open-weight coding models — and investing another $1 billion at a $12 billion valuation. Nvidia will also extend job offers to more than 100 Poolside employees, but Poolside's three co-founders stay in place and the company keeps operating independently. It's the second time in under a year Nvidia has used this structure, after a similar license-and-hire arrangement with Groq in late 2025.
Why it matters
Nvidia gets a model-building team's capabilities and future upside without the regulatory scrutiny or integration cost of a formal acquisition — a structure other chipmakers are likely to copy.
Broadcom seeks $60B+ in debt to finance AI chips for Anthropic
Broadcom is in talks with lenders including Blackstone and Apollo to raise more than $60 billion — potentially up to $100 billion including a junior debt tranche — to finance AI chip infrastructure that will supply Anthropic. Under the proposed structure, investors would finance the chip purchases and lease the hardware to Anthropic rather than Anthropic buying it directly, extending a financing partnership Broadcom, Blackstone, and Apollo struck in June.
Why it matters
Debt investors, not the AI companies themselves, are increasingly the ones fronting capital for frontier compute — a structure that keeps massive chip spending off Anthropic's own balance sheet while its IPO plans are still taking shape.
New assessment: no frontier AI lab meets basic AI-control standards
Guidelight AI Standards graded five frontier labs — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, and Meta — on six basic "control" practices, including logging internal AI activity, gating risky actions through review, emergency shutdown mechanisms, and containment plans for misaligned models. Anthropic and OpenAI tied for the top grade at C+, Google scored a D+, xAI a D-, and Meta an F. Labs did better on monitoring and third-party assessment than on actually preventing or containing risky AI behavior.
Why it matters
A best-in-class grade of C+ on labs' own internal AI-safety practices is a blunt reminder that the industry's public safety commitments are running ahead of what companies have actually implemented.
Colossal spinout Astromech raises $20M to build predictive AI models of biology
Astromech, an evolutionary-biology AI startup co-founded by Colossal Biosciences' Ben Lamm and George Church, raised $20 million at a $3.8 billion valuation, nearly doubling its worth from earlier this year. The company trains models on genomic and evolutionary data from species like cancer-resistant elephants and long-lived whales to predict how biological systems change and where they're vulnerable, with applications in conservation and disease prevention.
Why it matters
It's a reminder that the AI funding boom isn't confined to model labs and chips — investors are pricing niche scientific-AI applications at billion-dollar valuations well before commercial products exist.

Google's open Gemma models pass 1 billion downloads
Google disclosed that its open-weight Gemma model family has passed 1 billion cumulative downloads since launching in early 2024, with developers publishing more than 100,000 fine-tuned variants. Google marked the milestone by launching an "Awesome Gemma" GitHub repository to catalog community projects, and highlighted deployments running Gemma in orbit at NASA and satellite companies Satlyt and Starcloud for onboard image analysis.
Why it matters
It's Google's clearest public data point yet on Gemma's real-world reach, and a sign the open-weight strategy is generating an ecosystem Google doesn't have to build itself.

ChatGPT can now read and send Apple Messages on Mac
OpenAI added an Apple Messages integration to the ChatGPT desktop app on Apple silicon Macs, letting the assistant read, search, summarize, and draft iMessage, SMS, and RCS conversations, and send messages on a user's behalf after a confirmation prompt. The feature is live across ChatGPT plans and works within ChatGPT Work and Codex, but not in regular ChatGPT chats, and it doesn't support Intel Macs.
Why it matters
It's OpenAI reaching directly into a first-party Apple app to handle a daily task — the kind of default-app integration Apple has historically guarded closely for its own Apple Intelligence features.
Anthropic launches Claude Academy, a free course platform
Anthropic opened Claude Academy, a free learning hub with about 20 self-paced courses ranging from beginner introductions to Claude API and Claude Code deep dives, plus a course on Anthropic's own "AI Fluency" framework for working with AI. No credit card is required, and courses award certificates on completion.
Why it matters
It's Anthropic formalizing how it trains its own employees into a public onboarding funnel — a low-cost way to convert curious users into fluent, dependent ones.
