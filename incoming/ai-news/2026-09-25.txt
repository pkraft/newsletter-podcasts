Google. They are preparing to launch some prototype satellites. They are carrying their Trillium TPUs. That is the name of the chips. They are going into low Earth orbit. More than what a five-year space mission would deliver, they say. But the orbital flight is meant to confirm that. With real data. Not just guesses. This launch is the first step in something called Project Suncatcher. It is Google’s longer-term effort. They want to explore networked satellite constellations for AI compute. There are further tests planned for twenty twenty-seven. Why does it matter? Well. Terrestrial data centers are running into problems. Power constraints. Land constraints. Putting compute in orbit... it offers effectively unlimited solar power. And no fights over local cooling water. It is a real hedge against those limits. Not just a publicity stunt. And this flight... it is the first actual data point. On whether the hardware survives long enough to matter. ...
