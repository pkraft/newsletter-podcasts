So. Google deep mind. They have a thing called Dream-RSI. It helps agents get better at searching. They don't touch the model. The model stays the same. The agent goes out and searches for a solution. It records every single attempt. It builds a tree of what it did. Then... it stops. It replays those recorded attempts. It tests them against thousands of different search strategies. It doesn't call the model again. It doesn't check the answers again. It just simulates. It picks the strategy that worked best in the simulation. Then it goes back out and uses that strategy for the next real round. It's like... dreaming about your mistakes.
