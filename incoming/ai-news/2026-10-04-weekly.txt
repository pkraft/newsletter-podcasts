So. A bit of software plumbing. Jarred Sumner. The man who built Bun. He published a detailed accounting. It was on Monday. He used about fifty orchestrated Claude agent workflows. He ported the entire Bun runtime. It is five hundred and thirty-five thousand lines long. He moved it from Zig to Rust. It took about four months. It cost roughly one hundred and sixty-five thousand dollars in A-P-I tokens. It fixed one hundred and twenty-eight bugs. But. It needed nineteen fixes for subtle regressions. And eleven rounds of security review before it could ship. According to InfoQ, this is one of the first public cost sheets for a whole-codebase agent migration. The number that actually matters isn't the token bill. It is the verification overhead. Those nineteen regression fixes and eleven security rounds. That is the work that decides whether agent-driven rewrites are trustworthy at this scale elsewhere. ... Also on Monday. ElevenLabs shipped a version four speech model.
