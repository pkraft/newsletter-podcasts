... There is research out. From Vals AI and Anthropic. It is about multi-agent teams. You know the idea. Lots of agents. Working on the same thing. The pitch is that it makes things better. The data says... it mostly just makes things faster. Vals AI ran the benchmarks. They found the cost went up. A lot.
