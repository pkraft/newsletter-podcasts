Alibaba has released something called OpenCodeReview. It is open source. It runs under the Apache two point oh license. The idea is a hybrid approach. It pairs deterministic file and rule matching with AI analysis. The goal is to flag problems. Things like null pointer errors and seekwall injection. According to tests reported by InfoQ, it matched the precision of Claude Code. However, it used roughly one-ninth of the tokens. That is a significant reduction in resource usage. On the other hand, outside reviewers measured the recall. They found it to be as low as twenty percent. Why does this matter? Well. High precision at a ninth of the cost, with weak recall, describes a specific type of tool. It is a reviewer that rarely raises a false alarm. But it misses most real bugs. So, it is a cheap first filter. It is not a replacement for a full agentic review. The design is the interesting part. It lets rules do the cheap matching. It reserves the model for judgment. That is the part other tools are likely to copy. ...
