So. open A-I has shipped something. It is called GPT-Live-1. It went into their A-P-I on September tenth. It is a full-duplex voice model. Which means it listens and speaks at the same time. This is... technically impressive, I suppose. Callers can interrupt without breaking the conversation. It replaces the old pipeline. You know the one. Transcribe. Then respond. A bit like waiting for a snail to cross the road before you’re allowed to blink. The pricing is... modular. The voice layer costs five cents per minute. You pay for that. Then you separately pay for the reasoning model that does the thinking and tool calls behind it. open A-I suggests using GPT-6 Astra, Luna, or Terra, depending on how difficult the task is. It is all very... layered. Like a sandwich that has too many layers. They have added telephony support. Native transcripts come alongside the spoken responses. And there is keyword biasing for names and reference codes. Because apparently, getting a name wrong is a specific type of failure they wanted to fix. ChatGPT’s own Voice mode now runs on GPT-Live-1 for paid users. Free users get a smaller version. GPT-Live-1 mini. Because if you aren’t paying, you get the mini. It is a fair system. Why it matters... well. Full-duplex listening is the piece phone-based AI agents have been missing. It is not turn-based exchange anymore. It is... simultaneous. And open A-I is pricing it as a layer separable from the reasoning model underneath. This pushes the voice-agent market toward modular, stacked pricing. Instead of one bundled per-minute rate. It is all about the separation of concerns. Or costs. Depending on your perspective. The sources for this are open A-I’s own announcement on building natural voice experiences.
